    CEO only

    Google Drive & automation

    Everything is saved to your private Atraski People folder in Google Drive, which only you and the people you give HR access can open (HR can view the People and Hiring folders, nothing else; that updates automatically when you change someone's access): each person's documents and letters, punch logs, leave and claims registers, the employee register, hiring and exit records, and pay runs. Letters and emails go out from your Gmail. A small script you run once in your own Google account does the writing, so nothing else gets access to your Drive.

    1. Open script.new while signed in to the Google account that owns the folder.
    2. Paste the script: then replace everything in the editor and press save.
    3. Deploy → New deployment → type Web app → Execute as Me → Who has access Anyone → Deploy → allow the permissions Google asks for.
    4. Paste the Web app link (it ends in /exec) below and save. Then test it.
